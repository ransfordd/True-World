BEYOND THE ICEWALL

ARTICLE The True Word

BEYOND THE ICEWALL: Unseen Technologies, Electromagnetic Disruptions, and the Urgent Need to Awaken.

By; Eric Paddy Boso

In our age of digital triumph, where artificial intelligence, search engines, and satellite communications reign supreme, the illusion persists that humanity stands on the cutting edge of technological evolution. But what if we are only scratching the surface? What if the systems we so deeply trust?our radars, military defenses, scientific instruments, and even religious doctrines?are blind to a far more advanced reality already operating among us?

This is not a call to panic. It is a call to awaken.

The Limits of AI, Google & Modern Technology

The internet, Google, Bing, and even advanced AI have revolutionized the way we access and process information. Yet, ironically, these tools may also represent the very limits of our understanding. Per information I have, I estimated that the knowledge accessible through search engines covers less than 10% of what truly exists. The other 90%?the unseen layers of reality, higher-dimensional technologies, and ancient hidden knowledge?is veiled, either deliberately or by the sheer incapacity of our current tools to perceive them.

Search engines operate within data-fed frameworks, but what if the most crucial data exists outside electromagnetic frequencies, beyond the reach of silicon-based intelligence? The devices and detectors we currently rely on?phones, radars, telescopes, satellites?are all built to operate within the electromagnetic spectrum. That means their processing capacity is fundamentally restricted to what that spectrum reveals. They cannot perceive, decode, or interact with higher technologies that operate beyond it.

We may be unknowingly imprisoned in a matrix of convenience that blinds us from the real operations unfolding silently around us.

Spacecrafts Hovering but Outside Spectrum

There are crafts?enormous, technologically sophisticated crafts?that hover silently in our skies, sometimes as low as the height of high-tension electric poles. They do not emit sound in the way our aircrafts do. They appear and disappear beyond the visible spectrum, remaining undetectable to the human eye and standard electromagnetic equipment. These crafts don?t land on the ground; they seem to float in fixed spatial coordinates, yet they maneuver with precision unknown to modern aviation.

Descriptions I saw suggest these crafts are massive, nearly the size of a football field, with round or spherical engines mounted at the rear, unlike anything in conventional aerospace design. Engineers or operators within these crafts are humanoid in form, though with heightened sensitivities and awareness. The interior housed cubicle-like living quarters, advanced control rooms with millions of buttons, lights, and holographic screens, all functioning as part of a highly synchronized intelligence infrastructure.

These beings appear to be engineers of a higher dimension, not aliens as portrayed in Hollywood, but beings that walk among us, possibly undetected, orchestrating their missions silently within the folds of our reality.

Subtle Invasion of Our Electromagnetic Fields

A pattern has been observed: whenever these crafts descend close to the earth?s surface, even for brief moments, radio signals distort, lights flicker, and electricity surges or fails. Yet, we look for faults in our transformers,

dismissing the interference as ?technical issues.? The truth is, our electromagnetic environment is being penetrated by technologies we do not understand.

Electricity, radio waves, and the internet all exist within a limited slice of the electromagnetic spectrum. But these crafts, and the intelligence behind them, may be utilizing frequencies beyond gamma rays, beyond light, and perhaps outside the spectrum altogether. Thus, any attempt to detect them using conventional tools is futile?they are cloaked not in invisibility, but in dimensional misalignment.

Our current scientific equipment cannot process these frequencies. We are trying to interpret the language of stars using only candles and smoke signals. We must realize that our devices are inherently limited by their electromagnetic constraints, and as such, cannot collect, decode, or respond to the higher-level transmissions and presence of these advanced systems.

Even more concerning, these energy fields?when intensified or uncontrolled?may unintentionally interfere with commercial air travel. Modern aircraft rely heavily on GPS, radar, altitude sensors, and electromagnetic navigation systems. If these advanced forces can cause lights to flicker or disrupt electricity, it is not far-fetched to assume they could also trigger malfunctions in aircraft systems mid-flight, posing a severe safety risk. In a worst-case scenario, these electromagnetic disturbances could contribute to or even cause aircraft crashes, especially when flights intersect with zones of interdimensional activity.

Implications for National & Global Security

Imagine a scenario where a state like America or Russia (for research purposes), or any nuclear-capable nation, launches a missile to strike a distant target. What if, mid-flight, that missile's path is intercepted or disrupted by one of these interdimensional crafts or the energy fields they control? A weapon designed to strike at 1,000 miles could veer off course, landing anywhere?causing unintended destruction, mass casualties, and diplomatic collapse.

Now extend that possibility to passenger aircrafts, cargo planes, or military jets flying through affected zones. With interference, navigation could be compromised, pilots could lose control, and aircrafts could be misdirected or destabilized mid-air?resulting in tragedy without clear explanation.

Our security systems?air defense, missile tracking, radar, satellite surveillance?are all rooted in electromagnetic detection. They cannot account for phenomena that transcend this framework. If these unseen systems begin to intentionally or unintentionally interfere, both civilian and military aviation could be jeopardized. Chaos would follow?not from attack, but from our blind spots.

So the question arises: Do world governments know? And if they do, why haven?t these phenomena been disclosed? And if they don't, what does that say about the vulnerability of the systems in which we place our faith?

Are We Operating on Outdated Systems? Yes!

The shocking truth is this: the technologies we revere today are already obsolete. Our phones, radars, drones, and even AI systems are mere toys compared to the machinery described above. We are driving carriages in a world that already has interdimensional jets. We are outdated?and unaware.

This isn?t to discredit our scientific progress or the hard work of our global institutions. But it is to call attention to a blind spot so vast, it could cripple us. We are buried and blinded by religion, self-serving science, and limited technologies?systems that shape our worldview but restrict our perception. Religion, science, and governance?our modern-day trinity of control?often use fear, rigid laws, and curated truths to maintain order. Yet, these very structures may act as veils, shielding us from deeper, multidimensional realities that demand greater awareness, openness, and courage to face.

The Way Forward: Research, Readiness, and Responsibility

This article is not intended to invoke fear or suspicion. It is a message of awareness.

If governments and scientific institutions are aware of these phenomena, they must act transparently and update global security and communication systems accordingly. If they are not aware, then we, the people, must initiate research that goes beyond particles and algorithms?into energy, frequency, dimensionality, and consciousness.

We must stop placing all our trust in visible phenomena. We must redefine technology not merely as hardware and code, but as the interaction between awareness and matter, frequency and form, soul and system.

We urge global leaders, thinkers, scientists, spiritualists, and technologists: begin the work. Bridge the gap. Open the minds of your people.

Because something is happening?right above our heads, sometimes at arm?s length?and we cannot afford to remain blind.

If we are not allowed to get to the Ice wall, then the Ice wall must move towards us!!!
