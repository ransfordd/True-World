Breaking the Frequency Trap

ARTICLE The True Word

Breaking the Frequency Trap: How Religion, Science, and Medicine Keep You Sick and Disconnected from Your True Power"

By; Eric Paddy Boso

Humanity stands at a spiritual and biological crossroads. On one side is the current mainstream system ? a world ruled by organized religion, profit-driven science, and a healthcare industry that profits from perpetual illness. On the other side lies a hidden truth: that human beings are energetic and spiritual beings capable of healing themselves, transcending disease, and evolving far beyond what we?ve been taught. But this truth remains buried, censored, and disguised. Why?

Because if humanity collectively raised its vibrational frequency, the current global systems ? religion, science, and medicine ? would collapse. The people would awaken, self-heal, and no longer depend on the constructs that were built to keep them sick, fearful, and docile.

This article uncovers the carefully crafted illusion and why raising your vibration is not only your birthright ? it is your greatest defense against disease, manipulation, and early death.

1. Religion: The Spiritual Straitjacket

Religion was once a sacred path to enlightenment. Today, much of it functions as a control mechanism. While it teaches discipline and moral codes, it often suppresses spiritual awakening.

Instead of empowering individuals to access divine power within, mainstream religion teaches believers to outsource their power to distant deities, religious leaders, or apocalyptic promises. Energetic practices such as meditation, chanting, breathwork, third-eye activation, or energy healing are labeled "demonic" or "new age" ? not because they are evil, but because they awaken real power.

Keeping people vibrating at low frequencies like guilt, fear, shame, sin consciousness, and unworthiness ensures they remain disconnected from their spiritual DNA ? unable to heal themselves, elevate their minds, or escape the 3D matrix.

2. Science: The Gatekeeper of Reality

Mainstream science ? while responsible for many breakthroughs ? has become a prison of materialism. It denies everything that it cannot measure, label, or sell. It rejects the quantum field of consciousness, energy medicine, bioelectric healing, and non-physical intelligence ? even when evidence exists.

Why? Because modern science has been hijacked by funding, institutions, and corporations who dictate what can be researched, published, or taken seriously.

We are made to believe that health is only physical ? cells, organs, pills, and procedures ? and that healing must be external. But the human body is not just chemistry. It is energy, frequency, and vibration, as Tesla once said. ?And when your frequency rises, your cells respond? ? they become impervious to illness.

But this secret cannot be commercialized, and thus it is buried.

3. The Medical System: A Business of Sickness

Today?s medical industry thrives not by curing people, but by keeping them in cycles of chronic illness. The more pills you take, the more side effects you experience ? leading to more diagnoses, more treatment, and more prescriptions. It's a loop of dependency designed to enrich pharmaceutical empires, not liberate human health.

Have you noticed?

Medications rarely cure ? they suppress.

Natural cures are ridiculed, banned, or made illegal.

Every new pill comes with long-term side effects that damage your organs, your immunity, and your life force.

The average person dies not from disease, but from accumulated damage caused by medications, processed food, stress, and toxic environments ? all of which lower your frequency.

When your vibration is low, your immune system weakens, your aura becomes porous, and disease enters. But when you raise your vibration, sickness cannot live in your body. And that is the greatest secret hidden from the masses.

4. Low Vibration = High Vulnerability

Most people live in survival mode: worry, stress, fear, anger, resentment, and regret. These are low vibrational states that:

Disrupt your hormonal and immune systems

Create inflammation and cellular breakdown

Invite illness, depression, and fatigue

People trapped in these states are more likely to get sick, stay sick, and seek external cures.

Vibrations are real. Your emotions, thoughts, intentions, and beliefs emit measurable frequencies. A person vibrating at 200 (anger) is far more prone to illness than someone vibrating at 600 (peace) or 700 (enlightenment).

And yet, we are never taught to raise our frequency ? only to manage symptoms, treat diseases, or pray for mercy.

5. How to Raise Your Vibration and Heal

Raising your vibration is not mystical nonsense ? it is biological, spiritual, and energetic science. Here are practical tools:

Breathwork ? Oxygenation and mindful breathing elevate frequency, calm the nervous system, and align energy. Sunlight and Nature ? Absorbing light and grounding with the Earth recharges your energy field.Live, Organic Food ? Natural foods carry bio-photons that nourish your cells and detoxify your system.Emotional Healing ? Forgiveness, inner child work, and trauma release are frequency upgrades.Gratitude and Joy ? These are high vibrational emotions that supercharge the immune system.Meditation and Stillness ? These allow divine intelligence to flow through you, resetting your frequency.Energy Healing and Sound Therapy ? Frequencies can recalibrate your DNA and energetic blueprint.

Body Eating (fasting) and Getting rid of sin- And what does not serve your higher-self e.g.; addictions such as sex and drugs.Knowledge and Awakening ? The more you know, the more you unshackle yourself from the Matrix.

?Belief is the First Dose of Healing.? (Placebo effect) When patients truly believe a treatment will work?even if it's inactive?their body can begin to respond positively, reducing symptoms or improving conditions.

6. The Ultimate Truth: You Are the Medicine

The biggest lie ever told is that healing comes from outside you.The biggest secret ever hidden is that you are your own healer.

When you raise your vibration:

Disease struggles to survive in your body

You become a co-creator with the universe

Your mind clears, your energy restores, your spirit awakens

You break the cycle of dependency and reclaim your sovereign health

They don?t want you to know this. Because a population that heals itself is uncontrollable. It is unprofitable. It is free.

CONCLUSION: A Call to Frequency Revolution

The war on consciousness is real. The tools of that war are religion without power, science without spirit, and medicine without healing.

But you have the weapon they fear most: your vibration.

It?s time to rise above the illusion. It?s time to reject the systems that thrive on your sickness. It?s time to embody the highest version of yourself ? one that cannot be manipulated, poisoned, or kept small.

Because when you raise your vibration, you don?t just heal yourself ?you shatter the entire illusion.
