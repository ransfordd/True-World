Our Story

The True Word was birthed from a divine calling—a vision to restore the original truth that has been obscured by centuries of religious tradition and human interpretation.

Founded by Eric Paddy Boso, this movement emerged not as another institution, but as a living testament to the unfiltered Word of God. We are a collective of awakened souls, seekers, and believers who have heard the silent call of truth within their spirits.

We are not an organization—we are a movement. A movement that transcends geographical boundaries, cultural differences, and denominational lines. We are united by one common purpose: to remember and embody the True Word that Yahushua lived and taught.

Eric Paddy Boso

Our teacher and founder, Eric Paddy Boso, is a prophetic voice committed to unveiling divine truth and empowering believers to walk in their full identity and purpose.

Through years of faithful study, prayer, and divine revelation, Eric has been entrusted with the calling to restore the original teachings of Yahushua—teaching that goes beyond religious dogma to the core of spiritual awakening and transformation.

His ministry is dedicated to helping people remember who they truly are in Christ, break free from limiting beliefs, and step into their divine destiny with confidence and clarity.

Beginner Level

This stage is for those just beginning to question life, identity, and meaning. It lays the foundation for faith and truth, helping you gain the courage to look within and begin seeing beyond conditioning.

Participants gain the courage to look within and begin seeing beyond conditioning. You'll develop a foundation for deeper spiritual exploration.

The Disciple's Path

Theme: Awareness → Transformation

Intermediate Level

This stage is for seekers ready to go deeper — to confront fears, heal wounds, and begin living consciously. Transform awareness into meaningful change in your daily life.

1
Focus Areas
  • Deconstructing limiting beliefs
  • Healing emotional and generational wounds
  • Practicing conscious choices (speech, thought, lifestyle)
  • Learning to embody love and truth
2
Practices & Exercises
  • Guided meditations for inner healing and release
  • Forgiveness & gratitude rituals
  • Journaling prompt: "What patterns must I release to live authentically?"
  • Transformation challenge — adopt one new conscious habit weekly
Expected Outcome

Participants begin experiencing freedom, peace, and alignment with their true essence. You'll transform awareness into tangible change.

The Master's Path

Theme: Transformation → Impact

Advanced Level

  • Building communities of awakening

Practices & Exercises

  • Community service or mentoring initiative
  • Journaling prompt: "How can my life become a message of awakening?"
  • Advanced meditations on oneness and unity
Expected Outcome

Participants embody awakening, living as vessels of truth and transformation. You'll create real-world impact and lead others in their journey.
