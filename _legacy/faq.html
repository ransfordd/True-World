Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about The True Word, our teachings, and your spiritual journey

Everything you need to know about awakening, transformation, and connecting with our community.

1 What is Awakening?

Awakening is the process of remembering who you truly are beyond the layers of conditioning, fear, and illusion. It is not about becoming something new, but about uncovering the original essence of your being — the part that has always been connected to Source. Awakening is a journey, not a destination; some call it enlightenment, rebirth, or remembrance. In essence, it is the soul remembering its light in a world of shadows. It is not instant perfection, nor is it about escaping the world, but rather about living in it with higher consciousness. True awakening does not create superiority — it humbles you, aligning you with love, truth, and oneness.

2 What is The True Word?

The True Word is a spiritual awakening movement dedicated to reconnecting humanity to Source — thus restoring humanity's direct connection with the Most High through the life and teachings of Yahushua (Jesus). Unlike religion, which often imposes traditions and doctrines, we focus on awakening the divine truth already within you.

3 Is The True Word a religion or church?

No. We are not a religion, denomination, or church. The True Word is a return to the oldest truth — the living Word that Yahushua revealed before it was reshaped by institutions.

4 Why do you use the name Yahushua instead of Jesus?

We honor His Hebrew name because "Yahushua" means "Yah saves." It carries the depth of His divine mission, reminding us that He came not to build religion, but to reveal the eternal path of freedom.

5 Is this message against churches or faith traditions?

No. We do not condemn churches. Many have good intentions. But we invite seekers beyond walls, rituals, and fear-based traditions to rediscover the fullness of Yahushua's truth.

Q6. What does it mean to "awaken the divine within"?

It means remembering who you truly are and why you are here: a child of the Most High with divine light, purpose, and identity. Awakening is living from this truth instead of fear, guilt, or external control.

Q7. How is this different from traditional Christianity?

Traditional Christianity often emphasizes doctrines, guilt, and rituals. The True Word emphasizes:

  • Direct connection with the Most High
  • Freedom from man-made control
  • Rediscovery of your divine nature
  • Living the divine blueprint Yahushua revealed

Q8. Why do you say "Religion binds but Truth liberates"?

Religion can use fear and rules to keep people dependent on institutions. Truth liberates because it reveals that your divine connection has always been within you — and cannot be taken away.

Q9. What do you mean by "Truth Above Tradition"?

It means choosing the unfiltered Word of the Most High over doctrines or distortions. We honor what Yahushua lived and taught, not what has been reshaped for power, control, or division.

Q10. What role does scripture play?

We honor scripture as a sacred witness, but always read it through the Spirit. The written Word points to the Living Word — Yahushua Himself.

Q11. Who is The True Word for?

For seekers, the spiritually restless, and the chosen remnant — the ones who feel there is more to life than what religion has shown. If these words stir you, you are already awakening.

Q12. Do I have to leave my faith to follow this path?

No. This is not about abandoning faith, but deepening it. You can continue honoring your traditions while awakening to the original truth Yahushua lived.

13 How do I know if I am "chosen"?

If something in this message resonates deep within you—if you feel called, stirred, or unable to ignore the hunger for truth—that is the Spirit confirming you are among the chosen. Being "chosen" is not about pride, superiority, or belonging to an exclusive group. It is about recognizing a divine spark within you that refuses to stay asleep. There are signs that often reveal this calling:

  • A Deep Inner Restlessness – You cannot be fully satisfied by material success, routine religion, or shallow pleasures. Something inside you keeps whispering, "There is more."
  • Unexplainable Sensitivity – You feel things others overlook: the suffering of people, the corruption in systems, the emptiness in worldly pursuits. Your spirit is finely tuned.
  • A Hunger for Truth – You are not easily deceived by lies, hypocrisy, or manipulation. You search beyond the surface, seeking wisdom that aligns with your soul.
  • Feeling Different or Misunderstood – Even from childhood, you may have felt "out of place," like you don't fully belong to the world's patterns. This is not rejection—it is distinction.
  • Strong Intuition and Inner Knowing – You often sense what is true or false before evidence appears. Your spirit discerns beyond logic.
  • Unshakable Calling – You may try to run from your purpose, but life keeps pulling you back to spiritual awakening and service to humanity.
  • Tests and Trials – Chosen ones often face unusual struggles, betrayals, and challenges. These are not punishments but refinements—preparing you for higher purpose.
  • A Magnetic Pull Toward the Divine – Whether through dreams, visions, synchronicities, or encounters, you keep being drawn closer to God/Source.
14 How do I begin my journey with The True Word?

Start by opening your heart. Read our teachings, meditate on scripture with fresh eyes, and invite the Spirit to guide you. Awakening is not about instant perfection—it's a step-by-step return to your true self. Contact for more directions.

15 Do you believe everyone will awaken?

Not everyone will choose to. The message of The True Word is especially for those who are ready to listen, question, and walk the path of freedom—the remnant called out of conformity.
