<p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Are You Living in a Way That Prepares You for Eternity?</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">BY: Eric Paddy Boso</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">There is a question that pierces deeper than religion, status, or outward rituals. It reaches beyond your church attendance, your titles, or the good deeds you post online.Are you truly living in a way that prepares your soul for eternity?</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">It is not enough to profess faith with your lips if your life does not echo it in spirit and in truth. The real test of spiritual readiness is whether your daily decisions are drawing you closer to God?or quietly pulling you away.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Prepare to meet your God.? — Amos 4:12</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Eternity Isn't a Destination?It's a Direction</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Many view eternity as a distant, final stop. But in truth, eternity begins now. It is the direction of your heart, the trajectory of your soul, and the posture of your daily life.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Every thought you think, every word you speak, every choice you make is either:</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Preparing your soul to meet God,</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Or distracting you from that sacred moment.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Living for eternity means aligning your life with the values of heaven, even while walking on earth. It's not perfection God seeks?but a heart that returns to Him, again and again.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">5 Signs You Are Living with Eternity in Mind</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">1. You live with repentance, not regret.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">You've made mistakes?but you no longer sit in shame. You've learned that returning to God quickly is more powerful than pretending you never fell.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.? — Psalm 51:17</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">2. You seek purpose, not popularity.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Applause fades, trends die, but obedience echoes into eternity.You've stopped chasing validation from people and started seeking affirmation from the Spirit.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Am I now trying to win the approval of human beings, or of God— — Galatians 1:10</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">3. You love sacrificially.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">You forgive those who hurt you. You bless those who slander you. You choose peace over revenge.Why” Because you know God is watching, not just people.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you.? — Matthew 5:44</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">4. You steward what you've been given.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Time, resources, relationships, talents?you treat them as sacred trusts, not personal trophies.You understand that you are managing what belongs to God.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“So then, each of us will give an account of ourselves to God.? — Romans 14:12</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">5. You live ready?not just for the rapture, but for responsibility.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">You're not merely waiting for heaven; you're bringing a taste of it wherever you walk.Your actions reflect kingdom values?justice, mercy, humility, and faithfulness.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Be dressed and ready for service and keep your lamps burning.? — Luke 12:35</p>

                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Are you building God's kingdom?or only your own image?</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">If Jesus returned today, would He recognize your life as one of His own?</p>
