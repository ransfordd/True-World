                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">The Science Behind Kissing: More Than Skin Deep</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">When two people kiss, a cascade of chemical reactions takes place in the brain. Hormones such as oxytocin (the "love hormone"), dopamine (the "pleasure chemical"), and serotonin are released almost instantly. These chemicals are directly tied to feelings of happiness, affection, trust, and emotional bonding. That's why a kiss can lift moods, deepen attachment, and create lasting memories.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Kissing also reduces stress and anxiety. The brain produces less cortisol (the stress hormone), promoting a sense of relaxation and safety. This simple act becomes a form of therapy?nonverbal, intimate, and effective.</p>

                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Improves Oral Health: Saliva production increases during kissing, which can help wash away harmful bacteria and plaque.</p>

This iconic painting elevates a single moment of romantic connection into a universal symbol of love and humanity. Cloaked in gold, the figures of a man and woman embrace in a timeless union, both individuals yet deeply intertwined.</p>
