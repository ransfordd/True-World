<p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Sleeping with a Married Person Is Spiritual Death: A Heart-Centered Reflection</p>

                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">In a world increasingly driven by pleasure, impulse, and secrecy, certain actions are cloaked under culture, “consent,? or emotional justification. But spiritually, not every mutual decision is harmless — some can cost us our peace, our purpose, and even our destiny. One of the deepest, most damaging of these decisions is engaging in a sexual or romantic relationship with someone who is already married.</p>

                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Marriage, in its divine design, is not merely a social contract — it is a spiritual covenant before God. When you intrude upon this covenant, you are not just crossing lines between humans; you are opposing a union sealed by divine authority.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate.? — Mark 10:9</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">To sleep with someone who is married is to partner with a spirit of division and destruction. It is to become a thief of intimacy, trust, and spiritual covering. No matter how secret, sweet, or justified it seems, this act invites disorder into your life.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">2. It Opens Gates to Spiritual Bondage</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Sex is not just physical; it is deeply spiritual. The Bible describes it as a merging of souls — “the two become one flesh” (Genesis 2:24). Every time you engage sexually, you are exchanging more than pleasure; you are transferring energy, burdens, and spiritual history.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Sleeping with a married person ties you not only to them but also to their spouse's pain, their family's covenant, and their spiritual journey. You take on guilt that is not yours. You inherit warfare you were never meant to carry.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">This is how spiritual death begins: quietly, invisibly, yet deeply.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">3. It Triggers Generational Curses and Cycles</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Many who have slept with married people unknowingly set off patterns of betrayal, barrenness (physical or spiritual), or brokenness that follow their children and future relationships. The door opened may seem momentary, but its consequences echo for years.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">You cannot sow betrayal and expect loyalty.You cannot dishonor covenant and expect divine covering.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">“Can a man scoop fire into his lap without his clothes being burned— — Proverbs 6:27</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">4. It Drains Purpose and Delays Destiny</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Many people ask why they keep hitting roadblocks in life — spiritually stuck, relationally empty, or emotionally numb. What they don't realize is that soul ties from past adulterous encounters can choke spiritual clarity. It robs you of the voice of God, the energy to pursue your calling, and the freedom to love rightly.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">Purpose thrives in purity. Impurity suffocates vision.</p>

                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">When King David slept with Bathsheba — a married woman — it brought death, national disgrace, and generational consequences. God forgave David, but the cost was heavy.</p>
                    <p className="mb-4 text-base leading-relaxed">You may be forgiven, but it doesn't erase the damage.</p>
